# Presets de estilo — módulos Render + Lighting + Quality tail

Cada preset llena el slot STYLE (GPT) o el módulo de estilo final (Nano Banana). **No hay preset default: siempre se pregunta a el director** (referencia visual, descripción libre, o uno de estos). Con referencia/descripción libre, construir un módulo custom con esta misma forma de tres partes.

## 1. `fotorreal-anti-retoque`
- **Render:** `visible fine skin texture with natural pores, fine lines, subtle asymmetries, natural visible makeup with slightly uneven blending, slight natural sheen, matte-to-natural complexion, no digital smoothing, no beauty filter, no AI-airbrushed look` *(en NB, voltear los "no" a: "authentic unretouched skin with visible pores and natural texture")*. Anclas de imperfección opcionales: `a few faint freckles, a small mole`.
- **Lighting:** `soft diffused studio lighting without harsh reflections`.
- **Quality tail:** `natural anatomy, high-end but unretouched commercial photography style, cinematic realism, 35mm film grain, 4K quality, sharp focus on texture detail`.

## 2. `editorial-polished`
- **Render:** `flawless-but-natural skin with fine texture retained, soft dewy highlight on cheekbones`.
- **Lighting:** `editorial beauty lighting, soft key and gentle fill`.
- **Quality tail:** `high-end fashion editorial photography, magazine cover quality, cinematic realism, 4K`.

## 3. `3d-pixar` (el estilo de la referencia un personaje recurrente)
- **Render:** `stylized 3D character render, appealing exaggerated proportions, smooth subsurface-scattering skin, soft rounded features, detailed hair strands and cloth simulation`.
- **Lighting:** `soft global illumination, three-point studio lighting, gentle rim light`.
- **Quality tail:** `high-end 3D animation studio quality, octane-style render, clean neutral background, 4K`.

## 4. `anime-2d`
- **Render:** `clean anime illustration, crisp lineart, cel-shaded flat color with soft gradient shadows, expressive large eyes with detailed iris highlights, consistent character model-sheet style`. (Sin cláusulas de poros/piel — no aplican.)
- **Lighting:** `even flat lighting, soft ambient shading`.
- **Quality tail:** `high-quality anime key visual, clean vector-like linework, sharp, 4K`.

## 5. `game-concept`
- **Render:** `painterly game concept art, semi-realistic rendering, orthographic model sheet, clear silhouette, material callouts implied through detail`.
- **Lighting:** `neutral even concept-art lighting`.
- **Quality tail:** `professional character concept art, artstation quality, sharp, 4K`.

---

**Regla transversal (adultos):** para personajes adultos, sumar al render `mature adult bone structure, defined jawline and cheekbones, adult facial proportions` — el sesgo babyface de los modelos es corrección recurrente.

**Regla transversal (IP):** el personaje es siempre **original**; referencias de celebridades o IP existente se usan solo como vibra, construyendo una identidad distinta.
